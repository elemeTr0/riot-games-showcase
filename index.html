Riot Games is a global video game developer and publisher focused on creating unforgettable player experiences.

Riot Games is an American video game developer and publisher founded in 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill. The company was created with the goal of developing games built around long-term player engagement and strong communities.

Known for titles such as League of Legends and VALORANT, Riot has expanded beyond game development into esports, entertainment, and global competitive experiences. The company continues to support its games through frequent updates, new content, and community-focused events.
